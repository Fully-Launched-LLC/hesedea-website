Consider · Psalm 107:43

Consider.

We are calling the Hesedea golf line 'Consider' which finds its foundation in Psalm 107 verse 43. The discerning golfer has much to Consider: The lie, the wind, the distance, and many other variables! So does the discerning Christian.

Psalm 107 ends with a simple call to us: 'Whoever is wise, let him attend to these things; let them Consider the Hesed of the Lord.' Consider is the golf line from Hesedea, built around the same calling.

Performance apparel for the course that carries the quiet reminder of Hesed, God's loyal, covenant-keeping love that does not depend on a clean round or a perfect score. Not a slogan. A posture.

Every piece is cut and finished for the way that golf is ideally played: long walks, changing conditions, and the steady attention that the game requires. Our design stays understated, consistent with the rest of Hesedea, so that the meaning stays close to the wearer while also being quietly spoken to the group.

On the tee box, the wise player Considers what matters most. Wear Hesedea's Consider golf wear as a reminder to do the same and Change the Conversation!
