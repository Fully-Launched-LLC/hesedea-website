Meet the Board

The people behind the mark.

Hesedea started with one person’s conviction that what we wear can carry what we believe. Meet the team building it.

Brunswick, Georgia

Mark D. Johnson

Founder

Mark founded Hesedea out of a simple conviction: that what we wear can carry what we believe. HESEDEA, intertwined with a bold cross and a circle, symbolizes how one act of Hesed spreads to another…and another…until it comes full circle, and that circle starts with the people who put it on.

By day, Mark is an attorney in Brunswick, Georgia, where his career has been spent showing up for people in complicated moments: in the courtroom, in local government, and in the community. That same instinct, to serve quietly and faithfully, is what led him to start Hesedea: clothing built with the same intention as the word it’s named after.

Mark is a member of Wesley Church at Frederica, where he has served on the Administrative Board and the Staff-Parish Relations Committee. He currently chairs the Board of Trustees of Magnolia Manor, a ministry caring for older adults across Georgia, and has given his time to the Brunswick-Golden Isles Chamber of Commerce, the Glynn County United Way, and the University of Georgia National Alumni Association.

Outside of work and ministry, Mark is married to Ginny, and together they have two sons and two daughters. He is an IFR-rated private pilot, and when he’s not flying, you’ll find him on the golf course or on the slopes.

Atlanta, Georgia

Lucy Johnson

Board Member/Advisor

Lucy Johnson serves on the Board of Directors of Hesedea, where she contributes her experience in technology, financial systems, and business operations. By day, she is a Financial Systems Administrator at Hall Booth Smith, where she works with the firm’s financial technology, reporting, and business systems.

Lucy graduated from the University of Georgia in 2023 with a Bachelor of Business Administration in Management Information Systems from the Terry College of Business. While at UGA, she was a member of Kappa Alpha Theta and took part in the chapter’s philanthropic efforts supporting Court Appointed Special Advocates (CASA).

She remains involved with the University of Georgia through her support of the Terry College of Business and the Hartman Fund, and also supports the Knox Martin Foundation, attending its annual Night for Knox event.

In her free time, Lucy enjoys traveling, practicing yoga, skiing, and spending time with family and friends.

Athens, Georgia

Victor K. Wilson

Board Member/Advisor

Victor K. Wilson brings almost four decades of higher education leadership to Hesedea’s Board of Directors. As former Vice President for Student Affairs at the University of Georgia, Victor built a career as a strong advocate for students and student development, particularly the inclusion and success of students from historically underrepresented groups.

At UGA, Victor oversaw a Student Affairs division of 16 departments and 600 staff members dedicated to enriching student learning and supporting student growth. He has authored numerous scholarly articles and given presentations on race, ethics, crisis management, and staff development in higher education, and has held leadership roles with the National Orientation Directors Association (NODA) and the National Association of Student Personnel Administrators (NASPA).

Victor also serves as a board member for Sigma Phi Epsilon Fraternity, St. Mary’s/Trinity Health Care System, and Athens Academy. He received both his bachelor’s degree in Social Work and his master’s degree in Education from the University of Georgia, and retired from UGA in September 2023 after 40 years of dedicated service in higher education.

A trained vocalist, Victor enjoys music of all types, college football, reading, and has a keen interest in U.S. presidential history.

Jesup, Georgia

Amber M. Carter

Board Member/Advisor

Amber M. Carter serves on Hesedea’s Board of Directors, bringing a background in litigation, environmental law, and governmental affairs to the team. She is an associate at Gilbert Harrell, where she practices primarily within the firm’s litigation and environmental and regulatory practice groups.

Amber began her career with the Georgia Department of Natural Resources (DNR) in Atlanta after graduating summa cum laude from Georgia Southern University. She spent nearly a decade at DNR, ultimately serving as Deputy Director of Public and Governmental Affairs, where she advocated for the agency’s public affairs priorities.

After earning her law degree from Georgia State University College of Law, Amber practiced civil and administrative litigation in the Atlanta and Brunswick areas. At Gilbert Harrell, she draws on her governmental affairs experience and environmental background to guide clients through regulatory compliance matters and litigation, along with disputes arising in fiduciary, real estate, construction, and business contexts.

Originally from Villa Rica, Georgia, Amber lives in Jesup with her husband, Ryan, and their children, Ford and Lillian. A 2023 graduate of Leadership Wayne, she and her family are members of Jesup Church of God, where they direct the Hope Pantry food ministry. In her spare time, Amber enjoys playing tennis, antiquing, and embroidering clothing and accessories.

New York, New York

Piper O’Quinn

Board Member/Advisor

Piper O’Quinn serves on Hesedea’s Board of Directors, bringing a background in fashion merchandising and brand-building to the team. She is a co-founder of Key, a fashion brand designing key pieces for the modern closet.

A native of St. Simons Island, Georgia, Piper graduated from the University of Georgia with a degree in Fashion Merchandising. While in college, she and a group of friends founded As You Are, a nonprofit hosting Bible studies and worship nights for college women.

After graduation, Piper moved to New York City to build her career in fashion, working in wholesale for a designer fashion brand. More recently, she and her best friend launched Key, their own fashion brand centered on designing versatile, essential pieces for the closet.

Piper is passionate about helping people know Christ in any capacity, and about encouraging and empowering women in every area of their lives.
