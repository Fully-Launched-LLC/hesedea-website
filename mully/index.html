Every purchase gives back

Partnering with Mully Children’s Family.

Net proceeds from every Hesedea purchase support trusted ministry partners, beginning with MCF in Kenya.

Mully Children’s Family (MCF) rescues orphaned and vulnerable children across Kenya, surrounding them with hope, education, and the transforming love of Christ. It’s the first ministry partner we chose to stand behind, a natural extension of what hesed means to us: steadfast love, shown before it’s earned.

Hesedea exists to change the conversation, and giving back is part of that conversation from day one. Net proceeds from every piece we sell are set aside for MCF, no matter how small the first drop is.

Your purchase isn’t just clothing. Depending on the season, it might help fund a calculator, a hygiene kit, an exam fee, an internship, or a laptop for a student at MCF. Small, specific things that add up to an education and a future.

As Hesedea grows, so will the circle of who we’re able to give back to. MCF is where that circle starts.

One act of Hesed, passed on until it comes full circle.

About MCF

Hope, education, and family for Kenya’s vulnerable children.

Mully Children’s Family is a Kenyan-led ministry caring for orphaned and vulnerable children, providing housing, education, and a path toward independence, all rooted in the love of Christ.
