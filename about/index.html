Our story

HESEDEA, intertwined with a bold cross and a circle.

One act of hesed spreads to another… and another… until it comes full circle.

At the heart of HESEDEA is the beautiful Hebrew word, Hesed, which is incorporated into a beautiful logo on our clothing items. The signature logo is designed to change your wardrobe and change the conversation. HESEDEA, intertwined with a bold cross and a circle, symbolizes how one act of Hesed spreads to another…and another…until it comes full circle.

Hesed is a word that God uses to describe himself: steadfast love, kindness, mercy and covenant faithfulness that never fails.

Exodus 34:6–7 · Psalm 136 · Lamentations 3:22–23 · Micah 7:18 · Psalm 23:6

HESEDEA was born from a simple yet powerful truth: we are called to spread the word of what He Said through our clothing and apparel lines.

In a world full of noise, division and fleeting trends, we believe clothing can spark something deeper. Every item we create carries an invitation: to Change your Wardrobe and Change the Conversation.

HESEDEA is more than apparel. This is a movement of faith in fabric. We craft premium, comfortable clothing: hoodies, golf and polo shirts, tee shirts, hats, totes, beach towels and stickers, designed for everyday life and meaningful moments. Whether you are on the golf course, in the coffee shop, in the office, on the beach, or serving in your church or community, HESEDEA pieces remind you (and those around you) of what He Said.

Circle. Cross. One act of Hesed, passed on until it comes full circle.

Every purchase gives back

Building His kingdom, one purchase at a time.

Net proceeds from HESEDEA go to trusted ministry partners, beginning with Mully Children’s Family (MCF) in Kenya, rescuing orphaned and vulnerable children with hope, education, and the transforming love of Christ.

Your purchase from HESEDEA is more than a purchase of clothing. You are helping to buy a calculator, hygiene kit, exam fee, internship or a laptop for a student at MCF. You are helping to build the kingdom.
